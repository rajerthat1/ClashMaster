feat(ppo): pipeline the learner loop and measure its real floor
The collector resolved one packet per forward on a single thread: the model

- async_cluster_self_play: one `ppo-inference` thread with at most one batch in
  flight. The coordinator stages every completed receive during the forward,
  applies each result as it lands, and sends the whole staged set as the next
  batch; the 2 ms coalescing window is kept and also applied at the job

  share the model, the LSTM state table and the CUDA-graph cache. Per-actor

  reports are reproducible. Fail closed if the coordinator ever waits on an
  engine with no outstanding receive.

  round into decode / native step / tensorize / channel; `profile_real_collection.py`
  gains `--synthetic-model` (checkpoint contract at narrow widths, isolates the

  multi-segment frame totals.
